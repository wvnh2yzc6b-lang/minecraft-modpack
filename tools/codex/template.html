Playable race · demon

Demon player

Players who choose the demon race are drawn with this body instead of their own skin. It stays player-sized, so hitboxes and armor still fit. A helmet hides the cowl, and an equipped elytra hides the wings.

Wing flight

  • Jump while falling to take wing. It works like an elytra, and you don't need to wear one.
  • The wings add a gentle, constant push toward where you look, capped at a cruising speed well below rocket boosts.
  • Powered flight costs a little hunger. At 3 food or less the push stops and you glide.
  • On the ground the wings fold flat down the back. They open halfway in a jump and spread fully in flight.

How it moves

  • A quick downstroke and a slower upstroke, with the wingtip half-folded on the way up.
  • The wingtip trails the arm, and the stroke sweeps forward going down and back coming up.
  • Hard beats on takeoff and climbs, steady beats while cruising, swept-back gliding in a dive.
  • The cloak trails the wingbeat and flutters faster with speed.

Posted roles · every race

Workers and guards

Farmers, builders and guards keep a base running instead of marching with the army. They take up a post where you summon them and ignore the Commander's Baton. Sneak + right-click one with an empty hand to have it follow you, and again to post it where it stands. Each race dresses its own, and demon workers are imps.

RoleManaWhat it does
Farmer10Harvests ripe crops, melons and pumpkins within 8 blocks of its post, replants from the seeds it carries, tills dirt and grass near water, and carries the harvest to the nearest chest or barrel. Starts with 8 wheat seeds.
Builder15Surveys the buildings around its post (21×21 blocks, 3 down, 12 up) and puts back whatever enemies break or explosions destroy, lowest blocks first, using blocks from a nearby chest. Blocks you and your allies place or break update its plan instead of being undone.
Guard20Holds its post, walks a short patrol, and fights anything hostile within 14 blocks. On spotting an enemy it rings an alarm that sends allied troops within 16 blocks into the fight.

Farmers and builders never fight and run from enemies. Right-click a worker with seeds or blocks to hand them over. Config: workers.buildersNeedMaterials, workers.guardAlarmRadius.

Champion abilities

    %%CHAMPIONS%%

Morale and routing

  • Morale runs from 0 to 100. Taking damage costs 2.5 morale per point of damage.
  • Watching an ally die costs 12 morale (35 if it was a captain).
  • Morale recovers every second, scaled by race, with +4 more while a captain is nearby.
  • Below 20 morale and 40% health, a soldier routs: it drops its target and flees for 6s, then regroups with +35 morale. Dwarves, angels and hive soldiers never rout.

Managing your troops

  • Right-click your soldier with armor, a sword or an axe to equip it. Gear you give them drops when they die.
  • Right-click empty-handed to see name, health, morale, order and formation.
  • No friendly fire between allies, including arrows and towers (configurable).
  • Soldiers attack whatever hurts you and whatever you hit.
  • Vanilla monsters (except creepers) now target soldiers too.
  • Army cap: 40 soldiers per commander (configurable).
Commander's Baton

Orders and formations

The baton commands every soldier you own within 96 blocks. Right-click cycles orders, sneak + right-click cycles formations. Within a formation, each troop type gets its own ranks: shields, then spears, swords and captains, then archers, then healers.

Orders

  • Follow me: march in formation 2.5 blocks behind you, turning as you turn. Soldiers engage enemies within 10 blocks of their slot.
  • Hold the line: form up 3 blocks in front of where you stand, facing where you look. They only engage within 6 blocks of their post (3.5 in a shield wall).
  • Charge!: break ranks and attack the spot you're looking at (up to 64 blocks). No leash. Plays a war horn.
  • March (enemy only): advance in formation behind a leader, then charge within 14 blocks of the objective.

Formations

  • Battle Line: ranks of 8, 1.6 blocks apart.
  • Shield Wall: ranks of 10, tightly packed (1.05 apart), with the shortest leash.
  • Wedge: an arrowhead of growing rows (1, 3, 5…) to break lines.
  • Square: melee troops in a ring facing out, archers and healers in an inner ring.
  • Skirmish: loose, staggered ranks of 6, 3.2 blocks apart, against arrows and splash damage.
The summoning economy

Mana

Each player has a mana pool shown as a blue bar above the hotbar. Signing a recruit contract spends the role's mana cost, multiplied by your race's rate. Creative mode summons for free.

Sources

  • Mana Ore (stone and deepslate): 9 veins of up to 7 blocks per chunk, from bedrock to Y=80, in every Overworld biome. Needs an iron pickaxe. Drops 2–4 shards plus XP, and works with Fortune and Silk Touch. Can also be smelted or blasted.
  • Manabloom: a glowing crop for farmland (light 2, or 7 when ripe). Ripe plants drop 1–3 shards and seeds. Seeds drop from 5% of short grass, tall grass and ferns, or are crafted from a shard and wheat seeds. Villagers can plant them.
  • Enemy soldiers have a 35% chance to drop 1–2 shards, and Warlords drop 8.
  • Each won wave drops 2 + wave number shards.
  • Passive regen: +1 every 3s, but only up to 20.

Pool

  • Start: 50 of 100 mana. The starter kit adds 8 shards and 4 seeds.
  • Mana Shard: right-click for +10. Sneak to absorb as many as fit.
  • Mana Crystal: +25 maximum mana, permanently, up to 1000.
  • Mana and maximum are saved per player, survive death, and sync to the HUD on login, respawn and dimension change.
Diplomacy

Factions

Players can found factions with their own colors, alliances and wars. Seven computer-run war factions are hostile to everyone, including each other. Monsters are enemies of all.

Who fights whom

  • Same faction, or allied factions: allies. They never harm each other, and healers and shrines help them.
  • Factions at war: enemies. Their soldiers attack on sight.
  • Unaligned players and other factions: neutral. Soldiers retaliate if attacked.
  • Enemy warbands and monsters: always enemies.
  • Alliances need both leaders to agree. War is declared by one side.

Enemy raids

  • Every 10s each player has a small chance (12% per 5 minutes by default) that a warband of 4–7 soldiers spawns 40–48 blocks away and marches on them in formation.
  • The faction is picked by biome: a faction is 8× more likely in its homeland. In the Nether only the Burning Horde comes.
  • At most 16 enemy soldiers near any player.
  • Enemy soldiers drop War Marks, and captains drop extra.
Stronghold and waves

Tower defense

Place a War Standard to found a stronghold. It has 30 integrity. Attackers that reach it strike it (Warlords for 3 per hit), and at 0 it falls. Integrity refills after each won wave and slowly regenerates in peace.

Defenses

  • Arrow Tower: shoots enemies within 20 blocks every 1.25s (needs line of sight). Arrows deal 3 damage and pass through allies.
  • Arcane Spire: every 2.5s, erupts three evoker fangs beneath an enemy. Gives off light.
  • Healing Shrine: every 2s, heals allies within 8 blocks for 2. Gives off light.
  • Towers fight for their placer's faction.

Night sieges

  • At night, while the owner is within 64 blocks, there's a 20% chance every 30s for a single siege wave, at most once per in-game day.
  • Attackers spawn 36–44 blocks away, march in a wedge and attack the standard.

Wave campaign (War Horn)

  1. Sound the horn within 32 blocks of your standard. Wave 1 starts immediately, and a boss bar appears for everyone within 96 blocks.
  2. During a wave, the bar shows the wave number, the attacking faction, enemies remaining and standard integrity. It turns purple on Warlord waves.
  3. Clearing a wave pays 4 + 2×wave War Marks and 2 + wave shards, plus wave÷5 diamonds on every 5th wave. Integrity refills.
  4. A green countdown bar then gives you a 30s break before the next wave.
  5. Waves grow each time: shieldbearers, spearmen, swordsmen and archers scale with the wave number, healers join from wave 3 and captains from wave 2. Gear tier rises every 3 waves, and waves are capped at 40 soldiers.
  6. From wave 4, attackers split across 2 fronts, and from wave 8 across 3. Every 5th wave a captain becomes a Warlord: triple health, 1.45× size, +4 damage, heavy knockback resistance.
  7. Sneak + sound the horn to stand down after the current wave. Losing the standard ends the campaign.
Siege AI

Breaching

  • An enemy soldier that hasn't moved closer to its target or objective for 2 seconds counts as stuck.
  • It then picks the block in its way: ahead at head or foot height, or above it when the target is higher up.
  • It hacks at the block with crack animation and hit sounds. Time is about 1.5s per point of hardness (minimum 0.75s), so stone takes about 2s and an iron block about 7.5s. The block drops as an item.
  • It won't break blocks harder than 10 (obsidian, bedrock and reinforced blocks stop it), and it never breaks the War Standard itself.
  • Requires the mobGriefing game rule, can be turned off in the config, and respects protection mods.
  • Your own soldiers never break blocks.
